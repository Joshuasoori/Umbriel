## About
Umbriel is an AI-first onchain project being built around a public AI assistant, a transparent utility layer and a long-term privacy-first wallet architecture.

The project currently includes:
- Umbriel AI
- UMBR on Base Mainnet
- Umbriel Map, a planned Bitcoin Ordinals land and identity layer
- a growing community infrastructure
- a future non-custodial Umbriel Wallet
