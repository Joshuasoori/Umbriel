# Umbriel Map — Genesis Drop

## Status
**Design phase. Not yet for sale.**

This document defines the proposed launch architecture for the first public Umbriel Map release. Final pricing, mint mechanics and legal/compliance checks must be completed before sale.

## Collection
- Collection: **Umbriel Map — Genesis**
- Network: **Bitcoin Ordinals**
- Total Genesis collection supply: **100 Lands**
- Genesis Parent: 1 parent inscription
- Founder Core: 5 lands
- Proposed public release: **95 Lands**

The full 100-Land Genesis supply should be published before the first public sale. No hidden Genesis supply should exist.

## Value model

Umbriel Land should not rely on speculative price promises. Its value proposition should come from five visible layers:

1. **Bitcoin provenance** — a real Ordinals inscription with verifiable parent/child provenance.
2. **Location** — each Land has a permanent coordinate and sector in Umbriel Map.
3. **Rarity** — a fixed rarity class published before sale.
4. **Visual identity** — deterministic, recognizable land art tied to its metadata.
5. **Utility** — access to the Umbriel product ecosystem.

## Core utility: 12 months of Umbriel Founder Access

Each eligible Genesis Land is planned to carry a one-time **12-month Umbriel Founder Access entitlement** for its holder.

Founder Access is intended to provide an advanced AI assistant experience through voice or text on mobile and laptop.

Planned capabilities, subject to supported connectors and explicit user authorization:
- calendar, scheduling and project planning
- connected email workflows
- Telegram workflows
- WhatsApp workflows where supported
- customer discovery and lead organization
- CRM-style customer follow-up
- sales and product communication
- supported commerce/service connections such as Amazon
- natural-language workflow orchestration
- access to new Umbriel tools released during the active 12-month period

### Entitlement rules
- The 12-month clock begins only when the holder activates Founder Access.
- The entitlement belongs to the current eligible Land holder until activated.
- After activation, transfer behavior must be defined before launch: either entitlement stays with the activating account or remaining time follows the Land. **Recommended v1: activation binds the 12-month entitlement to the activating account** to prevent account/credential abuse.
- One Land activates one Founder Access entitlement unless explicitly stated otherwise.
- Third-party subscriptions, marketplace fees, phone/SMS costs or paid APIs are not automatically included.
- Umbriel never asks holders to expose seed phrases or private keys.

## Proposed 95-Land public release

Recommended public allocation:
- 10 Legendary
- 20 Epic
- 25 Rare
- 40 Uncommon

The 5 Founder Core Lands are recommended to remain outside the initial public sale for protocol/community use.

Total Genesis supply remains fixed at 100.

## Scarcity rules
- Exact total supply published before mint.
- Exact first-drop quantity published before mint.
- No fake countdown extensions.
- No undisclosed reserve.
- No artificial trades, wash volume or fabricated demand.
- Unsold Lands remain transparently unsold; they are not presented as sold out.
- Any team/community allocation is disclosed before mint.

## Location premium
Each Land receives immutable map coordinates, for example:

`RING-01 / SECTOR-014 / X12-Y34`

Location can create non-financial differentiation:
- proximity to The Core
- adjacency to Vault, Signal or Builder zones
- special visual landmarks
- future map experiences tied to sectors

Location advantages must be documented before sale if they affect utility.

## Pricing architecture

Do not choose an arbitrary BTC number first. Establish a sustainable price floor:

**Minimum Land price =**
- Bitcoin inscription cost
- marketplace/payment cost
- art/generation allocation
- expected 12-month AI service cost
- support/infrastructure reserve
- security/compliance reserve

Then apply transparent rarity multipliers.

Suggested relative multipliers:
- Uncommon: 1.0x
- Rare: 1.5x
- Epic: 2.25x
- Legendary: 3.5x

These multipliers are launch pricing mechanics, not claims about future resale value.

## Recommended sale mechanic
For the proposed public release:
- fixed public prices by rarity
- transparent quantity per tier
- no bonding curve in v1
- no hidden price changes during the announced window
- one clear official mint page
- wallet verification before mint
- optional per-wallet purchase cap during the first window

This is easier to understand and audit than a complex auction.

## Holder experience

After acquisition:
1. Land appears in a compatible Ordinals wallet.
2. Holder connects Bitcoin wallet to Umbriel.
3. Umbriel verifies ownership using a signed message; no seed phrase is shared.
4. Land dashboard displays ID, coordinates, rarity and provenance.
5. Holder can activate the 12-month Founder Access entitlement.
6. Umbriel account can optionally link other user-authorized services.

## Founder Access positioning

Do not market the Land as an investment contract or guaranteed-return asset.

Position it as:
**Bitcoin-native digital land + persistent Umbriel identity + one year of advanced Umbriel access.**

The product benefit should be understandable even if secondary-market prices never increase.
