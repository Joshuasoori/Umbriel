**Planning model. Not yet a public sale or price promise.**

## What a buyer receives

Each eligible Genesis Land is planned to include:

1. A Bitcoin Ordinals child inscription linked to the Umbriel Map Genesis parent.
2. Immutable Land ID, sector, coordinates and rarity.
3. Deterministic Umbriel Map artwork.
4. Holder identity/status inside the Umbriel ecosystem.
5. A one-time **365-day Umbriel Founder Access entitlement**, activated by the eligible holder.
6. Holder-only community access and selected early product previews.

## Founder Access economics

The Land price must not imply unlimited third-party spending.

Recommended product rule:
- Land includes the **software entitlement** for 365 days.
- Umbriel includes a defined fair-use AI allowance.
- Paid third-party products, phone/SMS charges, marketplace fees, premium APIs and subscriptions are not automatically included.
- If a user exceeds the included compute allowance, additional usage can later be handled through credits, UMBR utility, or a separate paid usage tier.
- Exact fair-use limits must be published before sale.

This protects the one-year promise while preventing a small number of extremely heavy users from making the product economically unsustainable.

## Internal reserve target

Before public launch, assign part of every sale to service delivery.

Recommended minimum internal planning reserve per activated Land:
- AI/model usage reserve
- server/infrastructure reserve
- support reserve
- security reserve
- connector/API reserve

A practical launch target is to keep **at least 35–45% of expected net Land revenue available for product delivery and operations** until real usage data exists.

This is an internal treasury discipline, not a claim about buyer returns.

## Why the first drop uses fixed prices

For v1, fixed prices are recommended over a bonding curve or dynamic auction because they are:
- easier for buyers to understand
- easier to audit
- easier to communicate
- less vulnerable to accusations of manipulated pricing
- compatible with transparent rarity tiers

No price should change secretly during the announced mint window.

## Purchase limits

Recommended initial mint rule:
- first access window: maximum 2 Lands per Bitcoin wallet
- later open window: remaining inventory may remove the cap
- team/reserve allocation disclosed before mint

This is intended to broaden access, not manufacture false scarcity.

## Activation rules for Founder Access

Recommended v1:
1. Holder connects the Bitcoin address controlling the Land.
2. Umbriel requests a signed ownership message.
3. No seed phrase/private key is ever submitted.
4. Holder chooses **Activate Founder Access**.
5. Activation starts a 365-day term.
6. The entitlement becomes bound to that Umbriel account.
7. Selling/transferring the Land afterward does not create a second 365-day entitlement.

Unactivated entitlement status must be visible to prospective buyers if secondary-market trading is supported.

## Price display

The mint page should display:
- BTC price
- sats price
- estimated local fiat equivalent for convenience
- network/inscription fee separately
- whether Founder Access is activated or unactivated
- exact included utilities

BTC/sats remain the canonical sale price.

## No speculative promises

Do not market these prices by claiming:
- guaranteed appreciation
- guaranteed resale demand
- guaranteed future floor price
- risk-free returns
- artificial countdowns or fake sold counts

The acquisition case should stand on:
**Bitcoin provenance + land identity + location + art + ecosystem access + 365-day Founder Access.**

## Repricing rule before launch

Before publishing final prices, recalculate:
1. current BTC price
2. current Bitcoin inscription/network fees
3. expected annual AI cost per active holder
4. hosting and connector costs
5. taxes/payment costs
6. legal/compliance costs

If BTC moves materially before launch, change the BTC price only before the public mint terms are frozen, not silently during the sale.
