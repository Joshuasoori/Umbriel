# Umbriel Founder Access — Land Holder Utility

## Goal
Turn Umbriel Map ownership into a useful product relationship rather than a purely collectible asset.

## Planned entitlement
An eligible Genesis Land can activate **12 months of Umbriel Founder Access**.

The service is designed as a conversational operating layer controlled through natural language on mobile or laptop.

## Planned capability areas

### Personal operations
- planning
- calendar coordination
- reminders and task workflows
- project organization

### Communications
With user authorization:
- email reading/drafting/workflows
- Telegram
- WhatsApp where supported
- contact follow-up

### Customer and sales workflows
- organize prospective customers
- research and prepare outreach
- track follow-ups
- draft proposals/messages
- help maintain CRM context
- support product sales workflows

### Commerce and connected services
Where supported by official connectors/APIs:
- Amazon and other commerce/service integrations
- order/product workflow assistance
- account-specific actions only with the user's permission

### Interaction
- text conversation
- voice conversation
- mobile
- laptop
- future Umbriel interfaces

## Security model
- no seed phrase collection
- no unrestricted access to user wallets
- OAuth or scoped credentials where available
- user-controlled connection revocation
- sensitive actions require explicit authorization
- financial signing stays with the user or an approved cryptographic policy

## Product truthfulness
The public site must distinguish:
- **Live now**
- **Beta**
- **Planned**

Land utility should only advertise a capability as available after it is working in production.

## Recommended activation model
For v1:
- ownership proves eligibility
- holder activates entitlement from Umbriel dashboard
- activation starts a 365-day term
- entitlement becomes bound to that Umbriel account
- transferring the Land after activation does not duplicate the benefit

A future collection can use renewable or transferable entitlements, but Genesis rules should remain stable after launch.
